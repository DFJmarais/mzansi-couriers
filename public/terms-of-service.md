- Driving record check
- Identity verification
- Address verification
- Vehicle inspection

### 4.2 Background Check Process

1. **Application**: Submit required documents
2. **Verification**: We verify your information
3. **Background Check**: Third-party conducts background check
4. **Vehicle Inspection**: Vehicle must pass safety inspection
5. **Approval**: You receive approval to start delivering

### 4.3 Disqualifying Factors

You will be rejected if you have:
- Criminal conviction (violent crimes, theft, fraud)
- Multiple traffic violations
- Suspended or revoked driver's license
- Outstanding warrants
- False information on application
- Vehicle that fails safety inspection

### 4.4 Ongoing Verification

- Background checks may be renewed periodically
- Driving record checked monthly
- Vehicle inspections required annually
- You must report any legal issues immediately
