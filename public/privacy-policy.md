# Privacy Policy - Mzansi Couriers

**Last Updated**: July 18, 2026  
**Effective Date**: July 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Mzansi Couriers ("we", "us", "our", or "Company") operates the Mzansi Couriers mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Registration Information:**
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Date of birth
- Government ID number
- Profile photo

**Payment Information:**
- Bank account details (for payouts)
- Payment method information
- Transaction history

**Delivery Information:**
- Pickup address
- Delivery address
- Package description
- Package weight/dimensions
- Special delivery instructions

**Communication Information:**
- Messages between customers and couriers
- Support tickets and communications
- Feedback and reviews

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Location Data:**
- Real-time GPS location (during active deliveries only)
- Location history (retained for 30 days)
- Delivery route information

**Device Information:**
- Device type and model
- Operating system version
- Device identifier (IDFA - with ATT permission only)
- App version
- Crash reports

**Usage Information:**
- Pages/features accessed
- Time spent on each feature
- Actions taken in the app
- Search queries
- Interactions with other users

**Network Information:**
- IP address
- Internet service provider
- Network type (WiFi/cellular)
- Bandwidth usage

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

**Payment Processor (Paystack):**
- Payment status
- Transaction verification
- Fraud detection data

**Mapping Services (Google Maps):**
- Route information
- Traffic data
- Geocoding data

**SMS/WhatsApp Provider (Twilio):**
- Message delivery status
- Phone number verification

**Background Check Provider:**
- Background check results (couriers only)
- Verification status

## 4. Data Retention

### 4.1 User Account Data
- **Retention Period**: For the duration of your account + 2 years after account deletion
- **Purpose**: Maintain account history, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations
- **Deletion**: Can be deleted upon request (see Section 11)

### 4.2 Location Data
- **Retention Period**: 30 days from delivery completion
- **Purpose**: Delivery tracking, route optimization, dispute resolution
- **Deletion**: Automatically deleted after 30 days

### 4.3 Payment Data
- **Retention Period**: 7 years (per PCI-DSS and tax requirements)
- **Purpose**: Payment processing, tax compliance, dispute resolution
- **Deletion**: Automatically deleted after 7 years

### 4.4 Delivery History
- **Retention Period**: 2 years
- **Purpose**: Dispute resolution, analytics, courier ratings
- **Deletion**: Automatically deleted after 2 years (can request earlier deletion)

### 4.5 Communication Records
- **Retention Period**: 1 year
- **Purpose**: Customer support, dispute resolution
- **Deletion**: Automatically deleted after 1 year

### 4.6 Background Check Data (Couriers)
- **Retention Period**: Duration of courier account
- **Purpose**: Safety verification, compliance
- **Deletion**: Deleted upon account termination

## 5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

### 5.1 Information We Share

**With Other Users:**
- Customer sees courier name, photo, rating, vehicle info
- Courier sees customer name, phone, delivery address
- Both see real-time location during delivery

**With Service Providers:**
- Payment processor (Paystack) - payment information
- Mapping service (Google Maps) - location data
- SMS/WhatsApp provider (Twilio) - phone number, messages
- Background check provider - ID information (couriers)
- Analytics provider - usage data (anonymized)

**With Legal Authorities:**
- Law enforcement (when legally required)
- Regulatory bodies (when legally required)
- Court orders or subpoenas

### 5.2 Information We Do NOT Share

- We do NOT sell your personal data
- We do NOT share data with advertisers
- We do NOT share data with marketing companies
- We do NOT share data with data brokers
- We do NOT share financial information (except payment processor)

## 6. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

### 6.1 Tracking Permission

When you first open the app, we request permission to track your activity across other apps and websites. This permission is optional.

**What we track (if you grant permission):**
- App usage patterns
- Feature interactions
- User preferences
- Crash reports

**What we track (regardless of permission):**
- In-app analytics (not linked to IDFA)
- Delivery performance
- Payment transactions
- Support interactions

### 6.2 Your ATT Choice

- **If you grant permission**: We use IDFA for analytics and personalization
- **If you deny permission**: We do not use IDFA and use alternative analytics methods
- **You can change this**: Go to Settings > Privacy > App Tracking Transparency

## 7. Security of Your Information

### 7.1 Security Measures

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption in Transit**: All data transmitted via HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
- **Encryption at Rest**: Sensitive data encrypted in database
- **Access Control**: Role-based access to data
- **Authentication**: Secure login with session management
- **Monitoring**: Continuous monitoring for suspicious activity
- **Incident Response**: Data breach notification procedure

### 7.2 Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach, we will:
1. Investigate the breach within 24 hours
2. Notify affected users within 48 hours
3. Provide information about the breach
4. Recommend protective measures
5. Comply with legal notification requirements

## 8. Your Rights & Choices

### 8.1 Access Your Data

You have the right to access all personal data we hold about you:
- Go to Settings > My Data > Download My Data
- Or contact us at privacy@mzansi.com
- We will provide your data within 30 days

### 8.2 Correct Your Data

You can update your personal information:
- Go to Settings > Profile > Edit Profile
- Or contact us at privacy@mzansi.com
- Changes take effect immediately

### 8.3 Delete Your Data

You can request deletion of your account and data:
- Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account
- Or contact us at privacy@mzansi.com
- We will delete your data within 30 days
- Some data may be retained for legal/tax purposes

### 8.4 Opt-Out of Marketing

You can opt-out of promotional communications:
- Go to Settings > Notifications > Marketing
- Or click "Unsubscribe" in any email
- Changes take effect within 24 hours

### 8.5 Limit Data Collection

You can limit how we collect and use your data:
- Disable location services (Settings > Privacy > Location)
- Disable camera/microphone (Settings > Privacy > Camera/Microphone)
- Disable app tracking (Settings > Privacy > App Tracking Transparency)
- Disable analytics (Settings > App Settings > Analytics)
