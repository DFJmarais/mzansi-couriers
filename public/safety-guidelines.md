# Safety Guidelines - Mzansi Couriers

**Last Updated**: July 18, 2026

## SAFETY FIRST - Your Safety is Our Priority

## 1. DRIVING SAFETY

### 1.1 Before You Drive
- Ensure your vehicle is in good working condition
- Check tires, brakes, lights, wipers
- Ensure fuel tank is adequate
- Ensure GPS/phone is charged
- Ensure you are well-rested
- Do not drive if fatigued, sick, or impaired

### 1.2 While Driving
- Follow all traffic laws and speed limits
- Drive defensively and anticipate other drivers
- Keep both hands on steering wheel
- Do not use phone while driving (use hands-free only)
- Do not eat or drink while driving
- Do not apply makeup or groom while driving
- Wear seatbelt at all times
- Ensure all passengers wear seatbelts
- Maintain safe following distance
- Do not drive under influence of alcohol or drugs
- Do not drive while taking medications that impair judgment

### 1.3 Accident Procedures
- If involved in accident:
  1. Ensure everyone is safe
  2. Call emergency services if anyone is injured
  3. Move vehicles to safe location if possible
  4. Call police and file accident report
  5. Document accident (photos, witness info)
  6. Contact Mzansi Couriers immediately
  7. Do not admit fault or discuss liability
  8. Provide insurance information to other party

## 2. PACKAGE SAFETY

### 2.1 Prohibited Items - DO NOT ACCEPT

**You must refuse delivery of:**
- Explosives or fireworks
- Weapons (guns, knives, etc.)
- Illegal drugs or controlled substances
- Alcohol (unless licensed)
- Tobacco (unless licensed)
- Hazardous materials (chemicals, batteries, etc.)
- Radioactive materials
- Biological hazards
- Perishable food without proper packaging
- Live animals (unless properly caged)
- Stolen goods
- Counterfeit items
- Items that are illegal to transport

### 2.2 Suspicious Packages

**Report suspicious packages immediately:**
- Package smells unusual (chemical, burning, etc.)
- Package is leaking or damaged
- Package is unusually heavy for size
- Package has suspicious labeling
- Customer behavior is suspicious
- Package feels like it contains weapons

**Action**: Do NOT transport. Contact support immediately.

**Emergency Contact**: +27 (0) 11 123 4567

### 2.3 Package Handling

- Handle packages carefully
- Do not drop or throw packages
- Secure packages properly during transport
- Do not open packages
- Do not tamper with packages
- Protect packages from weather
- Deliver packages promptly
- Obtain signature/photo proof of delivery

## 3. PERSONAL SAFETY

### 3.1 Safe Delivery Practices

- Inform someone of your delivery route
- Share your location with trusted contact
- Avoid delivering alone in unsafe areas
- Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, it probably is
- Do not enter homes or private spaces
- Meet customers in public areas when possible
- Do not accept deliveries to suspicious locations
- Cancel delivery if you feel unsafe
- Report safety concerns to support

### 3.2 High-Risk Situations

**Avoid or cancel delivery if:**
- Area has high crime rate
- Delivery is late at night in unfamiliar area
- Customer is acting suspiciously
- Location appears abandoned or dangerous
- You are being followed
- You feel threatened or uncomfortable
- Area is experiencing civil unrest
- Weather conditions are dangerous

### 3.3 Personal Items

- Keep valuables secure
- Do not leave vehicle unattended with valuables
- Keep phone charged
- Keep emergency contact information accessible
- Carry identification
- Carry emergency cash
- Do not display large amounts of cash
- Do not accept rides from strangers

### 3.4 Health & Wellness

- Get adequate sleep before driving
- Do not drive if sick
- Do not drive if taking medications that impair judgment
- Stay hydrated while driving
- Take breaks on long routes
- Maintain healthy lifestyle
- Seek medical attention if injured
- Report health issues that affect driving

## 4. CUSTOMER INTERACTIONS

### 4.1 Professional Behavior

- Be polite and professional
- Greet customers respectfully
- Maintain appropriate distance
- Do not enter customer homes
- Do not accept gifts or tips (unless policy allows)
- Do not discuss personal matters
- Do not share personal information
- Do not accept personal requests

### 4.2 Conflict Resolution

If customer is angry or aggressive:
1. Stay calm and professional
2. Listen to their concerns
3. Apologize if appropriate
4. Offer to resolve the issue
5. Do not argue or become defensive
6. If situation escalates, leave and contact support

**Do NOT:**
- Argue with customer
- Become aggressive
- Use profanity
- Make threats
- Refuse service without cause

### 4.3 Harassment or Abuse

If you experience harassment or abuse:
1. Remove yourself from situation
2. Contact support immediately
3. Document incident (date, time, details)
4. Provide witness information if available
5. File police report if necessary
6. Do not retaliate

**Support Contact**: +27 (0) 11 123 4567

## 5. VEHICLE SECURITY

### 5.1 Prevent Theft

- Lock vehicle when not in use
- Do not leave vehicle running unattended
- Park in well-lit areas
- Park near other people/businesses
- Remove valuables from vehicle
- Do not leave packages unattended in vehicle
- Use steering wheel lock or alarm
- Consider GPS tracker for vehicle

### 5.2 If Vehicle is Stolen

1. Contact police immediately
2. Provide vehicle details (make, model, license plate)
3. Contact Mzansi Couriers
4. Contact your insurance company
5. Do not attempt to recover vehicle yourself
6. Cooperate with police investigation

**Emergency Contact**: +27 (0) 11 123 4567

## 7. WEATHER & ENVIRONMENTAL SAFETY

### 7.1 Severe Weather

**Do not deliver in:**
- Heavy rain (visibility poor, roads slippery)
- Thunderstorms (lightning risk)
- Fog (visibility poor)
- Snow/ice (roads impassable)
- High winds (dangerous driving conditions)
- Extreme heat (vehicle overheating risk)

### 7.2 Weather Safety Tips

- Check weather forecast before starting
- Reduce speed in poor weather
- Increase following distance
- Use headlights in rain/fog
- Do not use cruise control in wet conditions
- Avoid flooded roads
- Report dangerous road conditions
