## 4. Data Retention

### Retention Periods

**Active User Accounts:**
- All data is retained while your account is active

**After Account Deletion:**
- Personal data is retained for **6 months** for legal and compliance purposes
- After 6 months, all personal data is permanently deleted
- Exceptions: Data required by law is retained as required

**Delivery Records:**
- Delivery records are retained for **6 months** for dispute resolution and analytics
- After 6 months, records are archived or deleted
- GPS tracking data is retained for 6 months for tracking and verification purposes

**Payment Records:**
- Payment records are retained for **7 years** as required by South African tax law
- Personal payment information is deleted after 6 months
