# 4 STEPS TO SUBMIT

## Step 1: Clean Build Folder
- Product → Clean Build Folder
- Wait for it to finish

## Step 2: Rebuild
- Product → Build
- Wait for it to finish (should see ✅)

## Step 3: Archive
- Make sure "Generic iOS Device" is selected (top-left)
- Product → Archive
- Wait (5-10 minutes)
- Organizer window opens

## Step 4: Upload
- In Organizer, click "Distribute App"
- Select "App Store Connect" → "Upload"
- Sign in with Apple ID
- Click "Upload"
- Wait (5-10 minutes)
- Done! ✅

---

**Total: ~30 minutes**

That's it! Your app is submitted! 🚀
