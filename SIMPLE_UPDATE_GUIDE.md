# 📋 Simple Update Guide - Copy & Paste Method

**Just like last time - copy 4 files and rebuild!**

---

## 📥 Step 1: Download the Files

Download these 4 files from the checkpoint:

1. **BridgeViewController.swift**
2. **AppTrackingTransparency.swift** (NEW FILE)
3. **Info.plist**
4. **capacitor.config.json**

Save them to your Downloads folder.

---

## 📂 Step 2: Open Your Xcode Project

1. Open Xcode
2. Open your existing Mzansi Couriers project
3. In the left panel, expand: **App** → **App** (the folder)

---

## 📋 Step 3: Replace Each File

### File 1: BridgeViewController.swift
1. In Xcode, find: **BridgeViewController.swift**
2. Right-click it → **Delete** → **Remove Reference**
3. Drag-drop the new **BridgeViewController.swift** from Downloads into that folder
4. Click **Copy items if needed** ✓
5. Click **Finish**

### File 2: AppTrackingTransparency.swift (NEW)
1. Right-click on the **App** folder in Xcode
2. Click **Add Files to "App"**
3. Select **AppTrackingTransparency.swift** from Downloads
4. Click **Copy items if needed** ✓
5. Click **Finish**

### File 3: Info.plist
1. In Xcode, find: **Info.plist**
2. Right-click it → **Delete** → **Remove Reference**
3. Drag-drop the new **Info.plist** from Downloads into that folder
4. Click **Copy items if needed** ✓
5. Click **Finish**

### File 4: capacitor.config.json
1. In Xcode, find: **capacitor.config.json**
2. Right-click it → **Delete** → **Remove Reference**
3. Drag-drop the new **capacitor.config.json** from Downloads into that folder
4. Click **Copy items if needed** ✓
5. Click **Finish**

---

## 🧹 Step 4: Clean & Rebuild

1. In Xcode menu: **Product** → **Clean Build Folder**
   - Wait for it to finish (30 seconds)

2. Then: **Product** → **Build**
   - Wait for it to finish (1-2 minutes)
   - You should see ✅ at the top

---

## 📦 Step 5: Archive

1. Make sure "Generic iOS Device" is selected (top-left)
2. Go to: **Product** → **Archive**
3. Wait (5-10 minutes)
4. Organizer window opens

---

## 📤 Step 6: Upload

1. In Organizer, click **"Distribute App"**
2. Select **"App Store Connect"** → **"Upload"**
3. Sign in with your Apple ID
4. Click **"Upload"**
5. Wait (5-10 minutes)
6. Done! ✅

---

## 🖼️ Step 7: Update App Store Connect

1. Go to: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
2. Sign in
3. Click **"My Apps"** → **"Mzansi Couriers"**

### Update App Icon:
1. Click **"App Information"**
2. Scroll to "App Icon"
3. Click on it
4. Select: **mzansi-app-icon.png**
5. Click **"Save"**

### Update Screenshots:
1. Click **"Previews and Screenshots"**
2. Delete old screenshots
3. Upload:
   - **appstore-screenshot-1-home.webp**
   - **appstore-screenshot-2-pricing.webp**
   - **appstore-screenshot-3-service-levels.webp**
4. Click **"Save"**

---

## ✅ Step 8: Submit

1. Click **"Submit for Review"**
2. Answer the questions
3. Click **"Submit"**
4. Done! 🎉

---

## ⏳ Timeline

- Copy files: 5 minutes
- Clean & rebuild: 5 minutes
- Archive: 10 minutes
- Upload: 10 minutes
- App Store updates: 10 minutes
- **Total: 40 minutes**

## 🆘 If Something Goes Wrong

**"Can't find the files"**
- Download them from the checkpoint again
- Make sure they're in your Downloads folder

**"Build failed"**
- Product → Clean Build Folder
- Try building again

**"Can't upload"**
- Check your internet
- Sign in again with your Apple ID
