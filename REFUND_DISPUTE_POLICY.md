## 1. Overview

This policy outlines how Mzansi Couriers handles refunds, disputes, and resolutions between customers and couriers. Our goal is fair, transparent, and efficient dispute resolution.

---

## 2. Refund Eligibility

### Refunds ARE Available For

**Delivery Not Completed:**
- Courier fails to pick up package
- Courier fails to deliver package
- Delivery is cancelled by courier without valid reason

**Courier No-Show:**
- Courier does not arrive within 1 hour of estimated pickup time
- No communication from courier

**System Errors:**
- Technical failures preventing delivery
- Incorrect fee calculation
- Payment processing errors

**Courier Cancellation:**
- Courier cancels delivery after accepting
- Courier cancels without completing delivery

**Significant Delays:**
- Delivery delayed more than 2 hours beyond estimated time
- Partial refund may be offered (at support discretion)

### Refunds ARE NOT Available For

**Customer Change of Mind:**
- Customer regrets booking after confirmation
- Customer wants to cancel after courier acceptance

**Recipient Refusal:**
- Recipient refuses delivery without valid reason
- Recipient is unavailable at delivery address

**Delivery Completed:**
- Package delivered successfully
- Recipient confirmed receipt
- No damage or loss reported

**Courier Delay (Minor):**
- Delays under 2 hours
- Delays due to traffic or weather (normal circumstances)

**Customer-Initiated Cancellation:**
- After courier has picked up package
- After delivery process has begun

## 3. Refund Request Process

### Step 1: Contact Support

- Submit refund request within **7 days** of delivery date
- Provide delivery reference number
- Describe reason for refund request
- Attach photos or evidence if available

### Step 2: Initial Assessment

- Support team reviews request
- Checks GPS tracking data
- Verifies delivery status
- Assesses eligibility

### Step 3: Evidence Collection

- Request statement from courier
- Request statement from customer
- Review in-app communication
- Analyze GPS tracking data

### Step 4: Decision

- Support team makes decision within 3-5 business days
- Decision is communicated to both parties
- Explanation is provided if refund is denied

### Step 5: Refund Processing

- Approved refunds are processed within 3-5 business days
- Refunds are issued to original payment method
- Confirmation email is sent to customer

## 4. Dispute Resolution Process

### Types of Disputes

**Delivery Disputes:**
- Package not delivered as promised
- Package damaged or lost
- Delivery to wrong address

**Payment Disputes:**
- Incorrect fee charged
- Duplicate charges
- Unauthorized transactions

**Conduct Disputes:**
- Courier misconduct or harassment
- Customer misconduct or abuse
- Safety concerns

**Quality Disputes:**
- Poor service or unprofessional conduct
- Vehicle condition issues
- Communication problems

### Dispute Initiation

**How to Report:**
1. Open in-app support chat
2. Select "Report Dispute"
3. Choose dispute category
4. Provide detailed description
5. Attach photos or evidence
6. Submit dispute report

**Timeline:**
- Disputes must be reported within 7 days of delivery
- Late reports may not be investigated
- Urgent issues can be escalated for immediate review

## 5. Investigation Process

### Automated AI Review

The system automatically analyzes:

**GPS Tracking Data:**
- Courier's actual route
- Pickup and delivery locations
- Route deviations
- Delivery confirmation location

**Delivery Timestamps:**
- Pickup time vs. estimated time
- Delivery time vs. estimated time
- Total delivery duration
- Delay analysis

**User Ratings:**
- Customer's historical ratings
- Courier's historical ratings
- Pattern analysis
- Fraud indicators

**Communication History:**
- In-app messages between parties
- Support interactions
- Complaint patterns
- Resolution history

### Manual Review

If AI confidence is low or dispute is complex, support team conducts manual review:

**Evidence Assessment:**
- Photos provided by parties
- Courier statement
- Customer statement
- Third-party evidence (if available)

**Credibility Analysis:**
- Party history and reliability
- Previous disputes
- Pattern of behavior
- Supporting evidence quality

**Liability Assessment:**
- Who is responsible for the issue
- Extent of damage or loss
- Applicable policy provisions
- Precedent cases

## 6. Resolution Options

### Option 1: Refund Approved

**Full Refund:**
- 100% of delivery fee returned
- Issued to original payment method
- Processed within 3-5 business days

**Partial Refund:**
- Percentage of delivery fee returned
- Used for minor issues or delays
- Amount determined by support team

**Courier Adjustment:**
- Courier's earnings are reduced
- Refund amount is deducted from courier payout
- Courier is notified of adjustment

### Option 2: Refund Denied

**Reasons for Denial:**
- Delivery completed successfully
- No evidence of damage or loss
- Customer change of mind
- Dispute outside policy scope

**Explanation:**
- Detailed explanation provided to customer
- Specific policy provisions referenced
- Evidence summary included

**Appeal Process:**
- Customer can appeal within 7 days
- Appeal is reviewed by senior support staff
- Final decision is binding

### Option 3: Partial Resolution

**Compromise Settlement:**
- Both parties share responsibility
- Partial refund offered
- Courier receives partial payment
- Both parties agree to resolution

**Replacement Delivery:**
- Original delivery is cancelled
- New delivery is arranged at no cost
- Customer and courier agree to retry

---

## 7. Appeal Process

### Appeal Eligibility

- Appeals must be filed within 7 days of initial decision
- Appeals require new evidence or information
- Frivolous appeals may result in account suspension

### Appeal Submission

1. Submit appeal through in-app support
2. Provide new evidence or information
3. Explain why decision was incorrect
4. Request specific outcome

### Appeal Review

- Senior support staff reviews appeal
- Additional investigation may be conducted
- Decision is made within 5-7 business days
- Final decision is binding and non-appealable

## 8. Special Cases

### Lost Packages

**Investigation:**
- GPS tracking confirms delivery location
- Recipient statement obtained
- Courier statement obtained
- Neighborhood investigation if possible

**Resolution:**
- If courier at fault: Full refund to customer, courier liable
- If recipient at fault: No refund, customer assumes risk
- If inconclusive: Partial refund offered

**Insurance:**
- If customer purchased insurance: Claim processed
- Insurance covers up to declared value
- Claim must be filed within 7 days

### Damaged Packages

**Assessment:**
- Photos of damage provided
- Courier statement obtained
- Packaging quality assessed
- Damage extent determined

**Responsibility:**
- If courier negligence: Courier liable
- If poor packaging: Customer liable
- If unclear: Partial refund offered

**Compensation:**
- Full refund if damage is severe
- Partial refund if damage is minor
- Insurance claim if applicable

### Delivery to Wrong Address

**Investigation:**
- GPS tracking confirms delivery location
- Recipient confirmation obtained
- Customer address verification
- Courier statement obtained

**Resolution:**
- If courier error: Full refund, redeliver to correct address
- If customer error: No refund, customer arranges pickup
- If system error: Full refund, redeliver at no cost

## 9. Fraud Prevention

### Fraud Detection

The system monitors for:
- Repeated refund requests from same customer
- Refund requests from same courier
- Unusual patterns or behavior
- Collusion between parties

### Fraud Consequences

**Customer Fraud:**
- Account suspension
- Refund denial
- Legal action if necessary
- Reporting to authorities

**Courier Fraud:**
- Account termination
- Earnings forfeiture
- Legal action if necessary
- Reporting to authorities

## 10. Chargeback Policy

### Paystack Chargebacks

- If customer disputes payment with Paystack, we will defend the transaction
- Evidence (GPS, photos, delivery confirmation) will be provided
- If chargeback is upheld, customer account may be suspended
- Repeated chargebacks result in account termination

### Chargeback Prevention

- Clear communication with customers
- Delivery confirmation and photos
- GPS tracking documentation
- Professional dispute resolution
