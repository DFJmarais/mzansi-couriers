# Mzansi Couriers - Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** June 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** June 1, 2026

---

## Overview

This Refund Policy explains when and how refunds are issued for deliveries made through Mzansi Couriers. Please read this carefully to understand your rights and responsibilities.

---

## 1. Customer Refund Policy

### 1.1 Refundable Scenarios

**Full Refund - 100%**

You are eligible for a full refund in the following situations:

**Delivery Not Completed:**
- Courier fails to complete the delivery
- Courier cancels after accepting the delivery
- Delivery is lost or not found
- Customer cancels before courier acceptance

**Courier No-Show:**
- Courier does not arrive within 30 minutes of scheduled pickup
- Courier does not respond to communication attempts
- Courier cancels without valid reason

**System Error:**
- Incorrect price charged
- Duplicate charge
- Technical glitch in the app
- Payment processing error

**Damaged Package:**
- Package arrives damaged or opened
- Package contents are missing or damaged
- Courier mishandles the package

**Partial Refund - 50%**

You are eligible for a partial refund in the following situations:

**Late Delivery:**
- Delivery arrives more than 2 hours late
- Delivery arrives after agreed time window
- Courier delays without communication

**Wrong Item Delivered:**
- Courier delivers to wrong address
- Courier delivers wrong package
- Courier delivers incomplete order

### 1.2 Non-Refundable Scenarios

**No Refund - 0%**

You are NOT eligible for a refund in the following situations:

**Change of Mind:**
- You change your mind after courier acceptance
- You no longer need the delivery
- You found a cheaper alternative

**Successful Delivery:**
- Package is delivered as agreed
- Package is delivered on time
- Package is in good condition

**Customer Cancellation After Acceptance:**
- You cancel after courier has accepted
- You cancel after courier has started pickup
- You cancel after courier is en route

**Customer Unavailability:**
- You are not available for delivery
- You do not answer calls or messages
- You do not open door for courier

## 2. Refund Process

### 2.1 How to Request a Refund

**Step 1: Contact Support**
- Open the Mzansi Couriers app
- Go to "Support" or "Help"
- Select "Request a Refund"
- Choose the delivery
- Explain the issue

**Step 2: Provide Evidence**
- Photos of the package (if damaged)
- Screenshots of communication
- GPS tracking data (available in app)
- Any other relevant information

**Step 3: Submit Request**
- Review your request
- Click "Submit"
- You will receive a confirmation email

**Step 4: Wait for Review**
- Our support team reviews your request
- We may contact you for more information
- We will make a decision within 5 business days

**Step 5: Receive Refund**
- If approved, refund is issued immediately
- Refund appears in your account within 3-5 business days
- You will receive email confirmation

### 2.2 Refund Timeline

**Request Deadline:**
- Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of delivery date
- Requests after 7 days are reviewed on case-by-case basis
- Late requests may be denied

**Processing Timeline:**
- Support review: 1-2 business days
- Refund approval: 3-5 business days
- Bank processing: 3-5 business days
- Total time: 7-12 business days

### 2.3 Refund Method

**Original Payment Method:**
- Refunds are issued to your original payment method
- If you paid by card, refund goes back to your card
- If you paid by bank transfer, refund goes to your bank account

**Refund Status:**
- You can check refund status in the app
- You will receive email updates
- Support can provide tracking information

## 3. Courier Refund Policy

### 3.1 Courier Earnings

**Earnings Are Final:**
- Once a delivery is completed, earnings cannot be refunded
- Earnings are paid out daily
- Earnings are non-refundable except in cases of fraud

**Exceptions:**
- Fraudulent delivery (not actually completed)
- Courier engaged in illegal activity
- Courier violated terms of service
- Chargeback or payment dispute

### 3.2 Courier Chargebacks

**If a customer disputes a charge:**
- We investigate the dispute
- We review GPS tracking data
- We review delivery photos
- We review communication history

**If chargeback is upheld:**
- Earnings for that delivery are reversed
- Amount is deducted from your next payout
- Your account may be flagged
- Repeated chargebacks may result in suspension

## 4. Dispute Resolution

### 4.1 If You Disagree with Our Decision

**Appeal Process:**
1. Contact support within 7 days of decision
2. Provide additional evidence or explanation
3. Our team will review your appeal
4. We will make a final decision within 5 business days

**What We Consider:**
- GPS tracking data
- Delivery photos and timestamps
- Customer and courier ratings
- Communication history
- Previous dispute history
- Evidence you provide

### 4.2 Escalation

**If You're Still Not Satisfied:**
1. Request escalation to management
2. Provide detailed explanation
3. We will conduct a full review
4. Final decision will be made within 10 business days

**Final Decision:**
- Management decision is final
- No further appeals available
- You may pursue legal action if desired

## 5. Special Circumstances

### 5.1 Lost or Damaged Packages

**Without Insurance:**
- Maximum refund: delivery fee only
- We are not liable for package contents
- You assume risk of package value

**With Insurance:**
- Maximum refund: declared package value
- Insurance covers loss or damage
- Claim must be filed within 7 days
- We will assess claim based on evidence

### 5.2 Force Majeure

**In Case of:**
- Natural disasters
- Severe weather
- Road closures
- Government restrictions
- Other unforeseen circumstances

**Refund Options:**
- Full refund of delivery fee
- Reschedule delivery for later date
- Store credit for future deliveries

### 5.3 Safety Issues

**If Delivery Poses Safety Risk:**
- Courier may refuse delivery
- Full refund will be issued
- No questions asked
- Courier safety is priority

## 6. Fraud Prevention

### 6.1 Fraudulent Refund Requests

**We investigate claims of:**
- Fake damage claims
- False non-delivery claims
- Duplicate refund requests
- Collusion between customer and courier

**If Fraud is Detected:**
- Refund will be denied
- Account may be suspended
- Legal action may be pursued
- Police may be contacted

### 6.2 Repeated Refund Requests

**Pattern of Refund Requests:**
- If you request multiple refunds in short time
- If you have high refund rate
- If your claims seem suspicious
- We may investigate your account

**Possible Actions:**
- Account suspension
- Refund denial
- Legal action
- Police report
