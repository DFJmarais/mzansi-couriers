# Roadie design research notes

Sources reviewed on 28 August 2026:

- https://support.roadie.com/support/s/article/How-Do-I-Track-a-Delivery
  Roadie gives each delivery a unique tracking number and supports real-time tracking through track.roadie.com. Relevant adaptation: make Mzansi tracking codes prominent and easy to use without login where appropriate.

- https://www.roadie.com/terms
  Roadie describes real-time tracking, photographic proof of pickup and delivery, sender dashboards, bulk delivery handling, and a driver app where drivers search for delivery opportunities and offer to perform them. Relevant adaptation: keep parcel state, route, proof photo, and courier offer/accept actions explicit.

- https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.roadie.drive.android.app&hl=en_US
  The current Roadie Driver listing emphasizes flexible work, choosing deliveries, seeing payment and mileage details before starting, and support for multiple vehicle types. Relevant adaptation: show Mzansi couriers the parcel area, vehicle/capacity fit, recorded earnings, and clear accept/decline decisions.

Design conclusion: Mzansi Couriers should borrow workflow clarity—not Roadie branding. Use a local warm-sand/deep-green/orange palette, clear parcel cards, route status, proof-of-delivery confirmation, Rand amounts, and South African address/context language. Avoid unverifiable income promises and avoid copying Roadie’s proprietary visual identity.
