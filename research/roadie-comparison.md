# Roadie comparison research

## Source reviewed
- Roadie Drivers official site: https://driver.roadie.com/
- Review date: 2026-08-28

## Product patterns found
Roadie’s driver-facing proposition emphasizes flexible scheduling, choosing individual or multi-stop deliveries, seeing exact pay up front, vehicle-specific opportunities, certifications for specialised work, and weekly or instant payout options. Its public onboarding guidance calls for driver identity/licence and banking information, followed by verification before delivery selection. Roadie also positions multi-stop routes and consolidated pickups as a way to make longer routes practical for larger vehicles.

Roadie’s customer and platform language emphasizes unique tracking numbers, real-time delivery tracking, delivery-status visibility, and a clear customer-facing tracking page. The driver site also makes earnings vary by distance, speed, and item size rather than promising a universal amount.

## Important adaptation limits
Roadie operates in the United States and uses U.S.-specific requirements and payment language. Mzansi Couriers should adapt the useful interaction patterns to South African Rand pricing, local addresses, owner approval, banking verification, Paystack weekly Thursday/Friday processing, and the project’s decentralized relay model. We must not copy Roadie branding, claims, testimonials, or U.S.-specific requirements.

## Initial gap hypotheses for Mzansi Couriers
1. Show the courier exact parcel earnings and route contribution before acceptance, where the persisted quote and courier share are available.
2. Show a simple daily parcel count and route sequence with accept/decline choices, including relay handoff context.
3. Add multi-stop planning only when real eligible parcels and route data exist; never fabricate opportunities.
4. Make payout history and transfer status easy to find, with weekly Paystack processing wording and verified-bank status.
5. Add clear eligibility/certification-style requirements for special vehicle or parcel types only if the backend supports them.
6. Preserve owner approval, proof-of-delivery photo, customer confirmation, and live tracking as the core Mzansi workflow.

## Additional official-source findings
Roadie’s support centre states that each delivery receives a unique 16-character tracking number and can be tracked in real time at its public tracker. The tracker page centers the experience on entering a tracking number and receiving minute-by-minute tracking updates. The iOS listing describes upfront visibility of payment, mileage, and other delivery details; choosing or skipping deliveries; multi-stop gigs; vehicle-specific RoadieXD blocks; in-app navigation preferences such as avoiding tolls or highways; and quick help options through an assistant. These are product patterns to evaluate, not claims to copy.
