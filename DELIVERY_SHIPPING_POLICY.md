# Delivery & Shipping Policy - Mzansi Couriers

**Last Updated:** May 2026

## 1. Overview

Mzansi Couriers is a peer-to-peer logistics platform that connects customers with independent courier contractors. This policy outlines the terms and conditions for delivery services.

---

## 2. Service Coverage

### Geographic Coverage

- **Service Area:** Entire South Africa
- **No Geographic Exclusions:** We operate nationwide
- **Expansion:** Future expansion to other African countries planned

### Service Hours

- Deliveries available 24/7
- Customers can book anytime
- Courier availability varies by location and time

---

## 3. Booking and Acceptance

### How to Book

1. Enter pickup and delivery addresses
2. Describe package (size, weight, contents)
3. Review estimated fee
4. Confirm payment method
5. Submit booking

### Booking Confirmation

- Booking is confirmed upon payment
- Customer receives booking reference number
- Estimated pickup time is provided
- Courier acceptance is not guaranteed

### Courier Acceptance

- Couriers voluntarily accept delivery jobs
- Acceptance is not guaranteed
- If no courier accepts within 30 minutes, booking is cancelled and refunded
- Customer can rebook immediately

---

## 4. Package Requirements

### Package Size Categories

**Small:** Up to 5 kg, fits in a backpack or small box  
**Medium:** 5-20 kg, fits in a medium box  
**Large:** 20-50 kg, requires special handling

### Package Specifications

- **Weight:** Must be accurately declared
- **Dimensions:** Must fit in courier vehicle
- **Packaging:** Must be secure and protected
- **Labeling:** Recipient name and address clearly marked

### Prohibited Items

The following items **cannot be delivered:**

- Weapons (firearms, explosives, ammunition)
- Illegal drugs or controlled substances
- Hazardous materials (flammable, toxic, radioactive, corrosive)
- Stolen goods or counterfeit items
- Perishable items without proper insulation
- Live animals
- Human remains or biological materials
- Items violating South African law
- Unlabeled or suspicious packages

**Violation:** If a courier discovers prohibited items, they may refuse delivery. The customer forfeits the delivery fee and may face account suspension.

---

## 5. Pricing

### Fee Calculation

Delivery fees are calculated based on:
- Distance between pickup and delivery
- Package size (small, medium, large)
- Package weight
- Current demand (surge pricing may apply)
- Time of day

### Price Display

- Estimated fee is shown before confirming booking
- Final fee may vary slightly based on actual route
- All fees are transparent with no hidden charges

### Platform Commission

- Mzansi Couriers retains **15% commission** from each delivery
- Remaining amount (85%) is paid to the courier
- Commission covers platform operations, support, and technology

### Optional Services

- **Delivery Insurance:** Available for additional fee
- **Priority Delivery:** Rush service with higher fee
- **Signature Required:** Confirmation of delivery by recipient

---

## 6. Delivery Process

### Pickup

**Timeline:**
- Estimated pickup time provided at booking
- Courier must arrive within 30 minutes of estimated time
- Customer must have package ready at pickup location

**Pickup Verification:**
- Courier confirms package details
- Photos may be taken for record
- GPS location is recorded

### In-Transit

**Tracking:**
- Real-time GPS tracking available to customer
- Customer can see courier location and ETA
- Courier communicates via in-app messaging

**Courier Responsibilities:**
- Handle package with care
- Follow traffic laws and safety regulations
- Maintain professional conduct
- Protect package from damage or loss

### Delivery

**Delivery Confirmation:**
- Courier arrives at delivery address
- Recipient signs or confirms receipt
- Delivery photo is taken
- GPS location is recorded

**Delivery Verification:**
- Recipient name confirmed
- Package condition assessed
- Delivery time recorded
- Customer receives notification

---

## 7. Delivery Times

### Estimated Delivery Times

- **Same-Day Delivery:** 2-4 hours (within city)
- **Express Delivery:** 1-2 hours (premium service)
- **Standard Delivery:** 4-8 hours
- **Next-Day Delivery:** 24 hours

### Delays

- Delays may occur due to traffic, weather, or other factors
- Courier will communicate delays via in-app messaging
- Delays do not automatically qualify for refunds
- Significant delays (>2 hours) may be eligible for partial refund

### No-Show Policy

- If courier does not arrive within 1 hour of estimated time, delivery is cancelled
- Customer receives full refund
- Courier may be penalized or suspended

---

## 8. Recipient Requirements

### Recipient Availability

- Recipient must be available at delivery address
- If recipient is unavailable, courier may leave package in safe location
- Courier will notify customer of package location
- Customer assumes risk if package is left unattended

### Signature and Confirmation

- Recipient must confirm receipt
- Signature or photo confirmation required
- Refusal of delivery must be communicated immediately

### Recipient Identity

- Courier may request ID verification
- Delivery is only to named recipient (unless otherwise authorized)
- Third-party receipt requires customer authorization

---

## 9. Damage and Loss

### Responsibility

**Courier Responsibility:**
- Couriers are responsible for safe handling and delivery
- Couriers must protect packages from damage during transit
- Couriers must report damage immediately

**Platform Responsibility:**
- Mzansi Couriers is a platform only
- We do not assume liability for lost or damaged packages
- We facilitate dispute resolution between parties

**Customer Responsibility:**
- Customers must accurately describe package contents
- Customers must use appropriate packaging
- Customers assume risk unless insurance is purchased

### Insurance

**Optional Delivery Insurance:**
- Available for additional fee at booking
- Covers loss or damage up to declared package value
- Claims must be filed within 7 days of delivery
- Claims are assessed based on evidence and courier statement

**Without Insurance:**
- No compensation for loss or damage
- Courier is responsible but may dispute claim
- Platform does not guarantee recovery

---

## 10. Refunds and Cancellations

### Cancellation Before Pickup

- Customer can cancel before courier accepts
- Full refund is issued immediately
- No cancellation fee

### Cancellation After Pickup

- Cancellation after pickup is not refundable
- Courier has already incurred costs
- Exception: Courier agrees to return package

### Refund Conditions

**Refund Eligible:**
- Delivery not completed
- Courier no-show (>1 hour late)
- System error or technical issue
- Courier cancellation

**Refund Not Eligible:**
- Customer change of mind
- Recipient refusal (without valid reason)
- Delay (unless >2 hours)
- Customer-initiated cancellation after pickup

### Refund Timeline

- Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of delivery date
- Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days
- Refunds are issued to original payment method

---

## 11. Dispute Resolution

### How to Report Issues

1. Contact support within 24 hours of delivery
2. Provide delivery reference number
3. Describe issue with details
4. Provide photos or evidence if available

### Investigation Process

**Automated Review:**
- GPS tracking data analysis
- Delivery timestamps verification
- Package condition assessment
- Communication history review

**Manual Review:**
- Support team investigates complex cases
- Both parties provide statements
- Evidence is evaluated
- Decision is made within 3-5 business days

### Resolution Options

- **Refund Approved:** Full or partial refund issued
- **Refund Denied:** Decision explained to customer
- **Appeal:** Both parties can appeal within 7 days
