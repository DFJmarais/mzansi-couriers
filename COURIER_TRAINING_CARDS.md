# Mzansi Couriers - Courier Training Module

## Complete Training Card Curriculum

All couriers must complete this 13-card training module during onboarding. Each card covers essential information about how to work with Mzansi Couriers.

---

## Card 1: Who We Are

**Title:** Who We Are

**Content:**
Mzansi Couriers is a peer-to-peer logistics platform. We connect senders with independent couriers like you. We use AI-powered dispatching to match jobs efficiently.

---

## Card 2: How It Works

**Title:** How It Works

**Content:**
Customers post delivery jobs. You accept jobs near you. You pick up the package, deliver it, and get paid. It's that simple.

---

## Card 3: Payment

**Title:** Payment

**Content:**
You earn per delivery. Payments are made weekly via Paystack. The more deliveries you complete, the more you earn.

---

## Card 4: Ratings & Reviews

**Title:** Ratings & Reviews

**Content:**
Customers rate you after each delivery. Maintain a high rating to get more job offers. Poor ratings can affect your access.

---

## Card 5: Pickup Checklist

**Title:** Pickup Checklist

**Content:**
1. Verify the package details match the order
2. Check for any visible damage
3. Get the sender's signature or confirmation
4. Take a photo of the package
5. Confirm pickup in the app

---

## Card 6: During Delivery

**Title:** During Delivery

**Content:**
1. Handle packages with care
2. Keep the package secure in your vehicle
3. Arrive on time - customers are waiting
4. Call the customer 10 minutes before arrival
5. Use GPS tracking in the app

---

## Card 7: Delivery Confirmation

**Title:** Delivery Confirmation

**Content:**
1. Get the customer's signature or photo ID confirmation
2. Take a photo of the package at delivery location
3. Get customer feedback if possible
4. Mark delivery as complete in the app
5. You're done - payment will be processed

---

## Card 8: Photo Requirements

**Title:** Photo Requirements

**Content:**
Always take clear photos:
1. At pickup - show package condition
2. At delivery - show package at destination
3. Include customer or location landmark
4. Photos help resolve disputes and protect you

---

## Card 9: Customer Communication

**Title:** Customer Communication

**Content:**
1. Be professional and polite
2. Respond to messages quickly
3. Call if you're running late
4. Ask for feedback to improve ratings
5. Never open or tamper with packages

---

## Card 10: Packaging Requirements ⚠️

**Title:** Packaging Requirements

**Content:**
**IMPORTANT: Packaging is the CUSTOMER responsibility.** Before pickup, verify the parcel is:
1. Securely packaged
2. Completely sealed
3. Clearly labeled with Parcel ID Code
4. In good condition

**If packaging is inadequate, REFUSE collection and notify customer to repackage.**

---

## Card 11: Customer Packaging Responsibility ⚠️

**Title:** Customer Packaging Responsibility

**Content:**
Customers MUST supply their own packaging materials (boxes, tape, padding, etc.). You are NOT responsible for providing packaging. Your role is to verify the package meets our standards before pickup. If a customer asks you to package items, politely decline and explain they must package themselves.

---

## Card 12: Parcel Identification Code ⚠️

**Title:** Parcel Identification Code

**Content:**
Every booking generates a unique Parcel Identification Code. Your job is to verify this code matches the package before pickup. The code must be clearly visible on the outside of the parcel. Refuse collection if the code is missing or does not match the booking. This protects both you and the customer.

---

## Card 13: Support

**Title:** Support

**Content:**
We're here to help. Contact support via the app for any issues. We respond within 24 hours.

---

## Training Completion

After reviewing all 13 training cards, couriers must:
- Confirm they understand all material
- Proceed to the Safety & Fraud Rules section
- Complete the onboarding quiz
- Provide bank account details
- Accept the Courier Agreement

**⚠️ Cards marked with warning symbols contain critical information about packaging responsibility and parcel verification. These are essential for preventing disputes and ensuring customer satisfaction.**
