# 🚀 Submit Your App NOW - 3 Steps!

**Manus already updated your Xcode project automatically. Just do this:**

---

## ✅ Step 1: Clean & Rebuild (5 minutes)

1. **Open Xcode** with your Mzansi Couriers project
2. Go to: **Product** → **Clean Build Folder**
   - Wait for it to finish
3. Go to: **Product** → **Build**
   - Wait for it to finish (should see ✅ at top)

---

## 📦 Step 2: Archive (10 minutes)

1. Make sure "Generic iOS Device" is selected (top-left)
2. Go to: **Product** → **Archive**
3. Wait (5-10 minutes)
4. **Organizer** window will pop up automatically

---

## 📤 Step 3: Upload (10 minutes)

1. In **Organizer**, select your archive
2. Click: **"Distribute App"**
3. Select: **"App Store Connect"** → **"Upload"**
4. Sign in with your **Apple ID**
5. Click: **"Upload"**
6. Wait (5-10 minutes)
7. You'll see: **"Upload Successful!"**
8. Click: **"Done"**

**Your app is now on Apple's servers!** ✅

---

## 🖼️ Step 4: Update App Store Connect (10 minutes)

1. Go to: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
2. Sign in
3. Click: **"My Apps"** → **"Mzansi Couriers"**

### Update Icon:
- Click: **"App Information"**
- Scroll to: **"App Icon"**
- Upload: **mzansi-app-icon.png**
- Click: **"Save"**

### Update Screenshots:
- Click: **"Previews and Screenshots"**
- Delete old screenshots
- Upload these 3 in order:
  1. **appstore-screenshot-1-home.webp**
  2. **appstore-screenshot-2-pricing.webp**
  3. **appstore-screenshot-3-service-levels.webp**
- Click: **"Save"**

---

## ✅ Step 5: Submit (1 minute)

1. Click: **"Submit for Review"**
2. Answer the questions (usually "No")
3. Click: **"Submit"**

**Done!** 🎉

---
